Prize bond draw schedule 2026

Next: 125th draw, scheduled 31 Oct 2026

Bangladesh Bank holds the 100 Taka prize bond draw four times a year: on 31 January, 30 April, 31 July and 31 October. When that date falls on the weekend (Friday or Saturday) or a public holiday, the draw is held on the next working day. For example, the 124th draw, scheduled for Friday 31 July 2026, was held on Sunday 2 August.
